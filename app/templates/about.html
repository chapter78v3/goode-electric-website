About Goode Electric

A family-owned and operated electrical company serving Birmingham, AL with pride and professionalism.

Our Story

Goode Electric is a family-owned business dedicated to providing honest, professional electrical services to the Birmingham community and surrounding areas. For over 20 years, we've built our reputation on quality workmanship and exceptional customer service.

What started as a passion for electrical excellence has grown into a trusted name in the industry. We're committed to treating every customer like family and every job with the same care and attention we'd give our own homes.

Whether you need maintenance, repairs, new installations, or a complete system upgrade, our experienced team is here to help. We take pride in our work and stand behind every job we do.

Serving Our Community
